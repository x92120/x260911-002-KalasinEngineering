# IS_Barriers_CAD — Pepperl+Fuchs IS Barrier Schematics

This folder contains AutoCAD `.dxf` CAD drawings for Pepperl+Fuchs Intrinsically Safe (IS) K-System Isolating Barriers used for hazardous area instrumentation in the **Ingredion Kalasin Spray Dryer Plant**.

---

## 📁 Drawing Inventory

* `PF_IS_Barrier_Wiring_Diagram.dxf` — Master Pepperl+Fuchs IS Barrier Wiring Schematic
* `PF_IS_Barrier_Sheet1_Wiring.dxf` — Sheet 1: Transmitter Isolator & Solenoid Barrier Wiring
* `PF_IS_Barrier_Sheet2_BOM.dxf` — Sheet 2: IS Barrier Panel BOM & Power Rail Layout

---

## ⚙️ Technical Details

* **Target Hardware**: Pepperl+Fuchs K-System (KFD2-STC4-Ex1, KFD2-SR2-Ex1.W, KFD2-CD-Ex1.32).
* **Generation Script**: `04_Automation_Scripts/generate_pf_is_barrier_dxf.py`.
