## 1. Product Overview

The **TAMCO CES Stainless Series (CES-STL)** is engineered for sanitary food & beverage, dairy, chemical, and demanding outdoor industrial environments where superior corrosion resistance and hygiene are required.

- **Material**: Stainless Steel Sheet AISI 304 (optional AISI 316L)
- **Protection**: IP55 Ingress Protection against dust and water jets
- **Sealing**: Continuous Polyurethane (PU) seamless gasket
- **Latching**: Stainless steel quarter-turn locking mechanism
- **Gland Plate / Flange**: Detachable bottom gland plate with gasket
- **Mounting**: Wall mounting brackets / direct rear enclosure mounting
- **Internal Plate**: Galvanized sheet steel sub-panel / mounting plate included
