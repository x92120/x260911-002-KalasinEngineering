> **Engineering Sizing Justification for IS-JB-603 (CES-STL-02)**:
> 1. **Terminal Strip Length**: 30 double-level terminals (5.2 mm each) + 2 end brackets + PE grounding clamp require ~185 mm mounting width.
> 2. **Wiring Ducts & Bending Radius**: Moving from original 200x300x150 mm to **300x450x150 mm** provides 50 mm clearance on each side for slotted wire ducts and generous 100+ mm top/bottom wire bending radius for 32 field instrument cables (16 DI + 16 AI).
> 3. **Intrinsically Safe (IS) Separation**: Meets IEC 60079-11 / IEC 60079-14 spacing requirements between IS terminal blocks and metallic enclosure sidewalls.
> 4. **Standardization**: Perfectly matches existing plant junction boxes `JB-402` and `IS-JB-618` (300 x 450 x 150 mm), streamlining spare parts and mounting brackets.
