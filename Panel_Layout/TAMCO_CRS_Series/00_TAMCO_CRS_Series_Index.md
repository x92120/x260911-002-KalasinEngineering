## 1. Product Overview

The **TAMCO CRS Series** is designed specifically for harsh outdoor environments, outdoor process structures (such as Baghouse, Cyclone decks, and rooftop platforms), and washdown areas where rain, water runoff, and dust are present:

- **Integrated Sloped Rain Canopy**: Top rain shield prevents water pooling and direct dripping over the door gasket.
- **Protection**: IP65 Ingress Protection against water jets and heavy dust ingress.
- **Door Construction**: Formed concealed hinges with PU seamless gasket seal.
- **Latching**: Push-to-turn waterproof cam lock with key.
- **Gland Plate**: Detachable bottom gland plate with sealing gasket.
- **Mounting**: Wall mounting brackets / rear mounting holes.
- **Internal Plate**: Galvanized zinc-coated mounting sub-panel included.
